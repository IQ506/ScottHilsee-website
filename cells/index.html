Cell Parts Study Aid

Learn the parts of animal and plant cells, then test yourself by labeling the diagram or finding each part from its name or its job.

Animal vs. plant cells

Plant cells only

  • Cell wall
  • Chloroplasts
  • Large central vacuole (animal cells have small ones, if any)

Both

  • Cell membrane and cytoplasm
  • Nucleus, nuclear envelope, nucleolus
  • Rough and smooth ER, ribosomes
  • Golgi apparatus and vesicles
  • Mitochondria

Animal cells only

  • Centrioles
  • Lysosomes (mainly; rare in plant cells)

Plant cells hold a fixed, boxy shape because of the wall; animal cells are rounder and can change shape. A common mix-up: plant cells have mitochondria too. Chloroplasts make sugar from sunlight, and mitochondria release the energy stored in that sugar, so plants need both.
